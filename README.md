# A-level Maths Past Papers

A complete mirror of A-level Maths past papers from [Physics and Maths Tutor (PMT)](https://www.physicsandmathstutor.com/a-level-maths-papers/).

**Total: 9,224 PDFs across all exam boards and modules.**

---

## Contents

### UK AS & A-levels (2017 spec)
- AQA — AS-level & A-level (Maths + Further Maths)
- Edexcel — AS-level & A-level (Maths + Further Maths)
- OCR (A) — AS-level & A-level (Maths + Further Maths)
- OCR (MEI) — AS-level & A-level (Maths + Further Maths)
- WJEC — AS-level & A-level (Maths + Further Maths)

### International A-levels
- **CAIE** — Pure 1/2/3, Further Pure 1/2, Mechanics 1/2, Statistics 1/2, Further Mechanics, Further Probability & Statistics
- **Edexcel IAL** — Pure 1–4, Further Pure 1–3, Mechanics 1–3, Statistics 1–3, Decision 1

### UK Modular (Old Spec)
- **C1, C2, C3, C4** — AQA, Edexcel, OCR, OCR-MEI, Solomon, Delphis, IYGB, Elmwood
- **S1, S2, S3, S4** — AQA, Edexcel, OCR, OCR-MEI, Solomon, Delphis
- **M1, M2, M3, M4, M5** — AQA, Edexcel, OCR, OCR-MEI, Solomon, Delphis
- **FP1, FP2, FP3** — AQA, Edexcel, OCR, OCR-MEI
- **D1, D2** — AQA, Edexcel, OCR, OCR-MEI, Solomon, Delphis

Each section includes **Question Papers (QP)**, **Mark Schemes (MS)**, and where available **Model Answers (MA)** and **Grade Boundaries**.
